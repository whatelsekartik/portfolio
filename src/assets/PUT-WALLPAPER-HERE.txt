Drop your wallpaper in this folder and name it exactly:

    wallpaper.png     (or wallpaper.jpg / wallpaper.jpeg / wallpaper.webp)

It is picked up automatically: no code changes needed.
Tip: 1920x1080 or larger, ideally under 2 MB. It is shown centered and scaled to cover the screen.
Once it exists, it becomes the default desktop and appears as "My Wallpaper" in Special > menu.
